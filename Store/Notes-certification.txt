NOTES FOR CERTIFICATION
(Partner Center › Submission › Submission options › Notes for certification — paste the text below, in English)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FluxTV Player is a media player for the user's OWN IPTV sources: an M3U playlist link or an Xtream Codes account provided by the user's TV service. The app does not provide, host, sell or link to any channel, movie or subscription.

HOW TO TEST WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
On the first screen, select "Essayer la démonstration" (Try the demo). No login is required. The demo loads:
- "Mire en direct": a public live test stream (Unified Streaming demo test card),
- "Ciné libre" and "Animation libre": free open movies streamed as continuous channels,
- 2 movies and 1 series made of free movies from the Blender Foundation (Big Buck Bunny, Tears of Steel, Creative Commons Attribution 3.0).

CONTROLS (Xbox controller)
- Left menu: Accueil (home), TV en direct (live TV), Films (movies), Séries (series), Rechercher (search), Réglages (settings).
- Live TV: select a channel to play it. During playback: A = controls, X = channel list, Y = audio and subtitles, up/down or LB/RB = previous/next channel, B = back.
- Movies / Series: open a title, then "Lecture" (Play). Left/right = seek 10 s, LB/RB = seek 30 s.
- Resume: stop a movie, it appears in "Continuer à regarder" (Continue watching) on the home screen.

CAPABILITIES
- internetClient: playlists, TV guide (XMLTV), images and video streams from the source chosen by the user.
- privateNetworkClientServer: many users run their playlist or TV server on their home network (e.g. http://192.168.x.x). The app only acts as a client; it does not listen for incoming connections.

PRIVACY
No personal data is collected. No analytics, no advertising, no account. The server address, username and password entered by the user are stored encrypted on the device (Windows Credential Locker / PasswordVault) and are only sent to the server chosen by the user. Privacy policy: see the URL in the Store listing.

CONTENT
Adult categories are hidden by default (Settings › "Masquer les contenus pour adultes").
