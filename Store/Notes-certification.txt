NOTES FOR CERTIFICATION
(Partner Center › Submission › Submission options › Notes for certification — paste the text below, in English)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FluxTV Player is a media player for the user's OWN IPTV sources: an M3U playlist link or an Xtream Codes account provided by the user's TV service. The app does not provide, host, sell or link to any channel, movie or subscription.

LANGUAGE
The app is in English and French. On first launch it asks for the language (the console's language is preselected); it can be changed anytime in Settings › "Language". The steps below use the English interface.

HOW TO TEST WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
After choosing the language, select "Try the demo". No login is required. The demo loads:
- "Live test pattern": a public live test stream (Unified Streaming demo test card),
- "Open Cinema" and "Open Animation": free open movies streamed as continuous channels,
- 2 movies and 1 series made of free movies from the Blender Foundation (Big Buck Bunny, Tears of Steel, Creative Commons Attribution 3.0).

CONTROLS (Xbox controller)
- Left menu: Home, Live TV, Movies, Series, Search, Settings.
- Live TV: select a channel to play it. During playback: A = controls, X = channel list, Y = audio and subtitles, up/down or LB/RB = previous/next channel, B = back.
- Movies / Series: open a title, then "Play". Left/right = seek 10 s, LB/RB = seek 30 s.
- Resume: stop a movie, it appears in "Continue watching" on the home screen.

ADD YOUR SOURCE FROM A PHONE (NEW IN 1.4)
Typing a long playlist link or password with a controller is tedious, so the setup screen offers "From my phone":
- The console shows a QR code. The phone (on the same home network) opens a small local web page served by the app itself, pastes its Xtream account or playlist link and taps "Send to the TV". The console then connects exactly as if the details had been typed with the controller, and the phone shows the result (connected, or the error to fix).
- The page only exists while this screen is open: it closes as soon as the source is added, when the user presses Back, after 15 minutes, or after 30 requests with a wrong code. Each opening uses a new random 6-character code in the address. It is reachable on the local network only (private IPv4 address of the console, port 8765); nothing goes through the internet or any FluxTV server.
- To try it: on the setup screen (first launch, or Settings › "Change source", pressed twice to confirm), choose "From my phone" and scan the QR code with any phone on the same network, or type the address shown under the QR code into the phone's browser. Sending a made-up account shows the error on both the phone and the TV.

CAPABILITIES
- internetClient: playlists, TV guide (XMLTV), images and video streams from the source chosen by the user.
- privateNetworkClientServer: many users run their playlist or TV server on their home network (e.g. http://192.168.x.x), which the app reaches as a client. It is also used by "From my phone" (see above): only while that setup screen is open, the app listens on the console's local network address so that the user's phone can send the source details.

PRIVACY
No personal data is collected. No analytics, no advertising, no account. The server address, username and password entered by the user are stored encrypted on the device (Windows Credential Locker / PasswordVault) and are only sent to the server chosen by the user. Privacy policy: see the URL in the Store listing.

CONTENT
Adult categories are hidden by default (Settings › "Hide adult content").

IN-APP PURCHASE (OPTIONAL)
One optional durable add-on, "Supporter Pack" (product ID "packsoutien"). It unlocks a gold color theme and a small supporter badge next to the logo; every feature of the app stays free. To see it: Settings (gear in the left menu) › "Support FluxTV" › the "Support FluxTV" button opens the Microsoft Store purchase dialog. Once purchased, a "Gold theme" switch appears in the same card.

RATING PROMPT (ONCE)
The app never asks for a rating during playback. Only once, after real use (at least 3 different days of viewing and 2 hours watched in total, outside the demo), when a playback of 10+ minutes ends normally, it shows a single card: "Enjoying FluxTV?" with "Rate FluxTV" (opens the Store's own rating dialog, StoreContext.RequestRateAndReviewAppAsync) and "No thanks". The user picks any rating in the Store dialog: the app does not suggest a rating, does not filter users and offers nothing in exchange. The card is never shown again. A permanent "Rate FluxTV" button is in Settings › "Support FluxTV".
