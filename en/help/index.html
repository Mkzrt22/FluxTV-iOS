FluxTV help

FluxTV and FluxTV Player (Xbox, Windows, Android, iPhone, iPad, Apple TV).

Contact us

A question, a problem or an idea: write to fluxtv.support@gmail.com. Answers in English or French, usually within a few days.

Does FluxTV provide channels?

No. FluxTV is a player: it doesn't provide, host or sell any channels, movies or subscriptions. It plays the sources you give it: only use it with streams you have legal access to. We can't help you find a playlist or a subscription.

Adding your source

At first launch, choose “Xtream account” (server address, username and password from your provider) or “M3U link” (your playlist address). To explore the app without a subscription: “Try the demo”.

A channel won't start or keeps cutting out

First check that the channel works with your provider. In FluxTV's settings, try another “Live format” or a longer “Buffer”. If your server is on your local network, your device must be connected to the same network.

The program guide is empty

The guide comes from your source (Xtream account, or your playlist's XMLTV address). If it stays empty, your provider may not offer it for these channels. In the settings, “Refresh lists” reloads everything.

Changing the language

In the app settings: “App language”, English or French.

Your data

FluxTV collects no data: your login details stay encrypted on your device. Details in the privacy policy.
