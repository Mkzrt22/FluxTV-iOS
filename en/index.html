Install on a TV box or a Fire TV
  1. Install the Downloader app (Amazon Appstore or Google Play).
  2. Allow it to install apps: Settings › My Fire TV › Developer Options › Install unknown apps (Google TV: Settings › System › About, click “Android TV OS build” 7 times, then Unknown sources).
  3. In Downloader, enter the address: mkzrt22.github.io/apk
  4. Install, then open FluxTV. Later updates arrive on their own.

If the short address doesn't work, the full one does: mkzrt22.github.io/FluxTV-iOS/fluxtv.apk. Phone or tablet: open this link in your browser and allow the installation. Fire TV devices running Vega OS (Fire TV Stick 4K Select, Fire TV Stick HD 2026) can't install Android apps.

FluxTV Home

Your TV box starts right on your channels.

FluxTV Home replaces the home screen of an Android TV, Google TV or Fire TV box. When you switch it on and every time you press Home, you land on your TV, not on ads.

New in 8.1: a completely redesigned home screen.

TV in the background

The selected channel plays full screen behind the home screen, muted. It stops as soon as a video starts: only one connection used.

Movies and series

Backdrop, synopsis, year, running time and rating for every movie. “Continue watching” picks up right where you left off.

Everything at hand

Your apps, search, the box settings, and channel numbers typed straight on the remote.

Ambient mode

After 4 minutes without touching the remote, your movie images drift by with a big clock.

  1. Install FluxTVFluxTV Home is built into the Android app (APK below).
  2. Turn it onIn FluxTV: Settings › Home screen › Use FluxTV as home screen, then follow the steps for your device.
  3. Fire TV and Google TVAmazon and Google force their own home screen: install the FluxTV Home Assistant (button in FluxTV, or below), then turn it on in Settings › Accessibility.

FAQ

Before you start.

Do I need an IPTV subscription?

Yes. FluxTV plays an Xtream Codes account or an M3U playlist from your provider. Without a source, demo mode lets you explore the app with a live test pattern and open movies.

Which languages is the app available in?

English and French, on every device. The language is asked at first launch (your device's language is suggested) and can be changed anytime in Settings.

Does FluxTV provide channels?

No. FluxTV is a player: it doesn't provide, host or sell any channels, movies or subscriptions. Only use it with sources you have legal access to.

Why “FluxTV Player” on the Microsoft Store?

The name “FluxTV” was already taken on the Store. It's the same app, with the same interface and the same demo mode.

Where do my login details go?

Nowhere but to your provider. They are stored encrypted on your device and only sent to your server. FluxTV collects no data.

How do updates work?

Xbox and PC: through the Microsoft Store. Android: when FluxTV starts, a small window offers the new version, which installs in one click (on Google TV, FluxTV can also update itself in the background). iPhone: download the new IPA and reinstall it with Sideloadly.

Do my favorites survive a change of provider?

Yes: favorites, resume points and history are tied to channels and movies, not to the server address.

Privacy

Privacy policy

FluxTV and FluxTV Player, on all devices — effective October 26, 2026.

In short

FluxTV collects no personal data. No FluxTV account, no advertising, no audience measurement, no tracking.

Your login details

Your server address, username and password (or your playlist address) are stored only on your device, encrypted by the system (Windows Credential Locker, Apple Keychain, Android encrypted storage). They are only sent to the server you chose, and are never shared with us.

Favorites and resume points

Favorites, history and resume positions stay on your device. They are deleted if you uninstall the app.

Network connections

FluxTV only connects to your source's servers (lists, program guide, images, videos), including on your local network. Demo mode uses public test streams (Unified Streaming, Mux) and open movies from the Blender Foundation. The Android version checks this website for updates.

Content

FluxTV is a media player: it doesn't provide, host or sell any content. You are responsible for the sources you add.

This website

The website anonymously counts visits and clicks on the download and support buttons, using the free Abacus service (abacus.jasoncameron.dev). Each visit adds 1 to a counter and to the one for the page's language, as well as to the counter of the network the link came from if it names one (for example “?s=reddit”). No cookies, no identifiers, no profiles: only a marker stays in your browser so the same visit or download isn't counted twice, along with your language choice. Like any website, this service and GitHub Pages see the connection's IP address. Donations are made on the donation platform, which has its own privacy policy. The apps themselves count nothing.

Children

The app collects no data, including from children. Adult categories are hidden by default.
